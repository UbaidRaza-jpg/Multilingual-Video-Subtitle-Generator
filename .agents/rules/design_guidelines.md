# Project Design Guidelines and Standards

To maintain authentic, high-grade engineering quality, the following standards apply across the codebase:

## Prohibited Patterns
- No purple gradients
- No spaceship buttons (neon glowing borders, intense colored drop-shadows, hovering glow effects)
- No fake reviews, testimonials, or fake metrics
- No fake hero text or hyperbolic marketing buzzwords
- No emoji icons
- No em dashes (use hyphens, colons, or parentheses instead)
- No over-the-top scroll animations, parallax jitter, or bouncy scroll triggers
- No AI slop photos or generic stock portraits
- No AI slop copy
- No custom cursor animations
- No fake customer or user counters

## Mandatory Launch Checklist
- Connect and configure custom domain
- Include and link custom favicon
- Remove any "Made with AI", "Powered by AI", or platform watermark badges
- Include accessible Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions pages
